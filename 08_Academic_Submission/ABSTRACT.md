# APEX AI: Research Abstract

## Abstract

Modern Formula 1 predictive analytics historically relies on static Saturday qualifying grid positions or uncalibrated historical averages, failing to capture in-race vehicle dynamics, transient micro-sector velocity profiles, track-specific aerodynamic downforce regimes, and non-linear tire thermal degradation. We present APEX AI, an end-to-end computational intelligence framework bridging physical telemetry modeling, gradient-boosted regression, intra-race discrete ordinal ranking, and autonomous agentic reasoning. APEX AI extracts micro-sector qualifying efficiency metrics, partitions circuits into four canonical downforce clusters, and deploys a 4-bit QLoRA fine-tuned Llama-3-8B ReAct agent with sandboxed Python and SQL tool-use capabilities. To eliminate look-ahead bias, we evaluate APEX AI via expanding-window walk-forward cross-validation across 64 Grand Prix events spanning the 2023–2025 FIA Formula One World Championship seasons. Across 49 sequential out-of-sample Grand Prix evaluation folds (857 verified finishes), APEX AI achieves a Mean Absolute Error of 2.191 positions, delivering a 19.16% relative error reduction over the status-quo grid baseline (MAE 2.711 positions) with decisive statistical significance (paired t-test t = 6.2559, p = 6.24e-10; Wilcoxon W = 75,599.0, p = 1.924e-09). In specialized tactical decision tasks, dedicated classifiers achieve 90.43% Podium accuracy (ROC-AUC 94.43%) and 75.22% Points finish accuracy (ROC-AUC 81.78%). Sub-second inference (14.2 ms machine learning latency, 1.62 s agent reasoning cycle) validates deployment feasibility under live race conditions. This research establishes an open-source, reproducible baseline unifying empirical vehicle telemetry with tool-augmented neural reasoning in high-velocity competitive environments.
